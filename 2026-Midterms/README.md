The goal of this model is to attempt to separate candidate effects from environmental effects in the US 2026 midterms

Questions:

1) How are races correlated nationally across states?
2) How are races correlated within states? (Senate, if present; House; Governor, if present; State House; State Senate)
3) How do these correlations hold at the county level?
4) How do the demographics of states and counties correlate with levels of correlation?
5) Does exit polling data match swings based on county demographics?












The objective of this is to give candidates WAR scores. WAR is made up of Senate WAR, House WAR, and Governor WAR based on a candidate's modeled impact across all races.

An important consideration is that H2H persuassion depends on the opposing candidate, as does turnout to an extent. It may be necessary to express WAR as a shared attribute of both candidates.
